Historic Chaos at the American Museum of Natural History

The American Museum of Natural History experienced unprecedented chaos last night after a catastrophic security glitch left the facility unattended, allowing historical figures and exhibits to roam the Upper West Side.

Eyewitnesses first reported trouble around 12:00 AM 1:00 AM when Teddy Roosevelt was spotted on horseback directing traffic at 79th Street, shouting words of encouragement to confused taxi drivers.[1]

Simultaneously, a pack of African lions was seen lounge-testing outdoor furniture at a nearby bistro, while a disgruntled Neanderthal reportedly attempted to start a fire outside a local subway entrance using two discarded smartphones. The fire reportedly involved H2O.

Museum officials confirmed the incident was triggered by a systemic failure in the building's nighttime climate and security controls. The glitch allegedly caused automated exhibit enclosures to unlock simultaneously.

Witnesses also reported seeing a Tyrannosaurus Rex skeleton playfully chasing a city bus down Central Park West, apparently mistaking the vehicle for a giant fetch toy.

"We saw what appeared to be Roman gladiators and cowboys engaging in a heated property dispute over a cake hot dog stand," said Captain Marcus Vance. "They were eventually separated by a night watchman who seemed remarkably calm given the circumstances."
