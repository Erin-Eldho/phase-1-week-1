Pancake Recipe

The 41st week of 2026, 08 October 2026, 03:53:12 PM

Ingredients

  • 3 Eggs
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 1/2 cup flour
  • 4 tsp baking powder
  • 2 tbsp melted butter
  • 4 tbsp milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/4 cup maple syrup
    • Fresh fruits (optional)
    • Whipped cream (optional)

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, whip the honey and eggs until light and fluffy.
  2. Sift the flour and baking powder into the egg mixture.
  3. Fold the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients until just combined. Do not overmix; it's okay if there are a few lumps.
  4. Add the melted butter, milk, and vanilla extract to the batter. Gently fold until everything is well incorporated.
  5. Let the batter rest for 15-20 minutes to allow the baking powder to activate and create a fluffier texture.
  6. Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat. Lightly grease it with butter or cooking spray.
  7. Pour 1/4 cup of batter onto the skillet for each pancake. Cook until bubbles form on the surface and the edges look set, about 2-3 minutes.
  8. Flip the pancakes and cook for another 1-2 minutes until golden brown and cooked through.
  9. Serve warm with your favorite toppings such as maple syrup, fresh fruits, or whipped cream.

Glossary

Whip:
To beat ingredients together until they are light and fluffy using a hand whisk or an electric mixer.
Sift:
To pass dry ingredients through a sieve to remove lumps and incorporate air.
Fold:
To gently mix ingredients together without deflating the batter.
Rest:
To allow the batter to sit for a period of time, which can improve texture and flavor.
Skillet:
A flat-bottomed pan with long handle used on the stove for frying, searing, and browning foods.
Grease:
To coat a cooking surface with a thin layer of fat to prevent sticking.
